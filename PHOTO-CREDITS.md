# Photo credits

Every photograph on these pages is real and licensed for commercial use. They are
**placeholders**: swap them for Set Right Homes' own job photos before launch, and this
file becomes unnecessary.

All files live in `/assets` and are self hosted. Nothing is hotlinked.

## What each licence requires of you

- **Public domain** and **CC0**: no attribution required. Use freely, including commercially.
- **CC BY**: commercial use allowed, **credit the photographer**.
- **CC BY-SA**: commercial use allowed, **credit the photographer**, and if you publish a
  modified version of the photograph itself you must share it under the same licence.
  Putting the unmodified photo on a web page does not trigger that.

The footer of each design links here. If you keep any CC BY or CC BY-SA image, keep a
visible credit somewhere on the site. The simplest option is a line on a credits or
terms page listing the photographer names below.
