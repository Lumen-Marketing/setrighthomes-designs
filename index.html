Before this goes to the client

Every phone number, address, licence number and figure is a placeholder. Search any file for SWAP: to find them. The phone is a 555 number so it cannot dial a real person, and the reviews are three invented customers. Direction 04 also advertises a free lot walk, so confirm that is an offer the business actually makes.

The photographs are real and licensed for commercial use, self hosted, nothing hotlinked. They are still stand ins for Set Right Homes' own job photos. Several are CC BY or CC BY-SA, which means the photographer has to be credited on the site if you keep them. Using the client's own photos removes that entirely. Licences are listed in PHOTO-CREDITS.md.

The forms do not send anywhere yet. All five validate and show a success state, but they need pointing at an inbox before launch.
