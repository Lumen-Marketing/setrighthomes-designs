Who we are

Set Right Homes is a site work crew. We do the three jobs a manufactured home needs before anybody turns a key, and we do all three ourselves instead of subbing them out and hoping the dates line up.

  • One contract, one schedule. No gap between the grader finishing and the plumber starting.
  • We pull the permits. Submittal, corrections and every inspection call handled by us.
  • Pads get tested, not eyeballed. Compaction results go in the file with the plan.
  • Rural lots welcome. We drive out to ground most crews will not, and the haul is priced up front.

Dirt work and site prep

Clearing, cut and fill, then an engineered pad compacted and tested to the detail your county signs off on.

  • Lot clearing and grubbing
  • Rough grade, finish grade, compaction testing
  • Drainage, swales and pad protection
  • Driveway approach and culvert

Utility connections

Water, sewer or septic, power and gas. Trenched, laid, pressure tested and inspected before the home shows up.

  • Water line and meter set
  • Sewer tie in or new septic
  • Electric service, pedestal and panel
  • Propane stub and final hookups

Setting the home

Placed, blocked, leveled, anchored and closed up. Singlewide, doublewide or triple, on piers or a permanent foundation.

  • Toter or crane placement
  • Pier, footing and anchor layout
  • Marriage line close up and leveling
  • Skirting, steps and final grade
