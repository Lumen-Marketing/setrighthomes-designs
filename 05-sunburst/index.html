What we do

Most lots need four contractors. We are one.

Hiring a grader, a plumber, an electrician and a set crew separately is where schedules fall apart.

Dirt work and site prep

Clearing, cut and fill, then an engineered pad compacted and tested to the detail your county signs off on.

  • Lot clearing and grubbing
  • Rough grade, finish grade, compaction testing
  • Drainage, swales and pad protection
  • Driveway approach and culvert

Utility connections

Water, sewer or septic, power and gas. Trenched, laid, tested and inspected before the home shows up.

  • Water line and meter set
  • Sewer tie in or new septic
  • Electric service, pedestal and panel
  • Propane stub and final hookups

Setting the home

Placed, blocked, leveled, anchored and closed up. Singlewide, doublewide or triple, piers or permanent.

  • Toter or crane placement
  • Pier, footing and anchor layout
  • Marriage line close up and leveling
  • Skirting, steps and final grade

Raw lot to keys, in five moves.

1

Walk the lot

Soil, slope, setbacks, and where the utilities actually stub out. Free, and usually an hour.

2

Plans and permits

Engineered pad detail drawn, county submittal filed, inspection dates on the calendar.

3

Dirt work

Clear, cut, fill, compact, test. Driveway and drainage go in while the machines are on site.

4

Utilities in the ground

Trench, lay, pressure test, inspect, backfill. Nothing gets covered before it is signed.

5

Set and tie down

Placed, blocked, leveled, anchored, marriage line closed, skirting and final grade.
