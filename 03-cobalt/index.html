Utility connections

Water, sewer or septic, power and gas. Trenched, laid, tested and inspected before the home shows up.

  • Water line and meter set
  • Sewer tie in or new septic
  • Electric service, pedestal and panel
  • Propane stub and final hookups

Setting manufactured homes

Placed, blocked, leveled, anchored and closed up. Singlewide, doublewide or triple, on piers or a permanent foundation.

  • Toter or crane placement
  • Pier, footing and anchor layout
  • Marriage line close up and leveling
  • Skirting, steps and final grade

A manufactured home is not a stick build.

It arrives on a steel frame, moves with the ground, and gets inspected against HUD rules. Contractors who do this once a year get it wrong in predictable ways.

The pad has to be right first

Soft fill under a pier shows up as a stuck door eighteen months later. Compaction gets tested, not eyeballed.

Anchors are engineered, not guessed

Spacing comes off the setup manual and your county wind zone, and an inspector checks both.

Utilities get signed before backfill

Trench open, pressure held, inspector on site. Covering a line early is the most expensive shortcut on the job.
