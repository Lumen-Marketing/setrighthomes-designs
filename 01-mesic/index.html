Manufactured Homes Are Not Site Built

A manufactured home arrives on a steel frame, moves with the ground, and is inspected against HUD rules rather than framing rules. Set Right Homes is a site work crew, not a general remodeller. We do the three jobs a home needs before anybody turns a key.

Contractors who do this once a year get it wrong in predictable ways. Soft fill under a pier shows up as a stuck door eighteen months later. Anchor spacing comes off the manufacturer's setup manual and your county wind zone, and an inspector checks both.

Why Manufactured Homes Require Specialized Site Work

Manufactured homes sit on ground that has to be built for them. Because of that, general excavation crews unintentionally cause structural problems, failed inspections, or finance issues. Set Right Homes exists to solve those problems.

Pad and pier supportLoad has to be spread correctly to prevent sagging, cracking and door failure.
HUD and lender standardsWork must meet HUD guidance to pass inspection, refinance or sell.
Settlement and levelingArizona soil shifts. That requires specialised compaction and drainage.
Tie downs and anchoringWind resistance and long term stability depend on proper anchoring.
Utility connectionsWater, sewer, septic and power all function differently under a manufactured home.
We know what inspectors look for and what lenders require in Arizona.
Work is built around protecting the structure, the safety and the long term value of the home.

How A Job Runs

1. Walk the lotSoil, slope, setbacks and where the utilities actually stub out. Free, and usually about an hour.
2. Plans and permitsEngineered pad detail drawn and stamped, county submittal filed, inspection dates on the calendar.
3. Dirt workClear, cut, fill, compact, test. Driveway and drainage go in while the machines are already on site.
4. Utilities in the groundTrench, lay, pressure test, inspect, backfill. Nothing gets covered before it is signed off.
5. Set and tie downHome placed, blocked, leveled, anchored, marriage line closed, then skirting and final grade.
