A manufactured home is not a stick build.

It arrives on a steel frame, it moves with the ground, and the inspection list is written by HUD, not by your framer. Contractors who do this once a year get it wrong in predictable ways.

The pad has to be right first

Soft fill under a pier shows up as a stuck door eighteen months later. Compaction gets tested, not eyeballed.

Anchors are engineered, not guessed

Spacing comes off the manufacturer's setup manual and the wind zone for your county, and an inspector will check both.

Utilities get inspected before backfill

Trench open, pressure held, inspector on site. Covering a line early is the most expensive shortcut on the job.
